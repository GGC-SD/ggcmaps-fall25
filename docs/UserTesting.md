### Date: 11-23-2025
**Tester:** Mike Deiters

#### Area Tested:
- Whole Site Review

#### Comments:
- Header is too tall. It is over powering
- (Personal opinion) I think the animation are maybe doing a little too much or something, they feel a little jarring to me and could use some tweaking. It might be the "lifting up" aspect
- The helpful links popup is a bit odd
  - There are 2 headers
  - The container is taller than necessary
  - The container inside of the popup container is a strange UI choice
- **Spanish Mode**
  - Much better positioning, not distracting like before
  - Could use a bit of spacing between the toggle and the text
- **Explore Campus**
  - Just like helpful links, I think the container inside of the container is an odd choice
- **Campus/Building Maps**
  - The name indicator is covering part of the map on load, I think it should be positioned better
  - Not the end of the world, but would be nice if the user had the ability to "to go" a particular floor, instead of always having to press/tap on the up/down arrows to get to the desired floor. (Probably not a highly used feature, so also kinda whatever)
  - Zooming is not centering on either the mouse cursor for scroll zoom or screen center for button zoom
  - Button based zoom controls blend into the map depending on the zoom and map position
  - As you zoom in more, the panning slows down. Meaning if I am zoomed in a lot, then I have to move my mouse a lot to pan just a little bit
- **Search**
  - Room Name search, at least in the E building, highlights the room, but the text remains white and not legible
    - Also "Game Room" should be searched as "Game Room" not "gameroom", or at least should also work
  - Did the auto complete feature break, or was that not implemented?
- **Footer**
  - GGC does not have copywrite, control, or authority over GGC Maps, remove this text
- **Legend**
  - Popup close button is not in a consistent position with other popups
  - Also has the issue with container inside of container
  - The hover/highlight is unecessary, and would give the impression that the user can click/tap on them to do something
- My previous feedback on the search/help buttons remain unchanged
- I like the UI layout of the help popup, use this layout for the other popup
- **Mobile**
  - Building indicator position is better than Desktop
  - Always priority icons over text on mobile, also FAB buttons never have text on them
  - Based on previous user research we have done, I imagine users would not see the search bar because you have the search/help buttons below the text input. Not a good UX
  - Floor selection is probably too small on mobile, but a little big on desktop
  - Popups should span the full width of the screen on mobile
    - Building selection does look less likely to suffer from fat fingering
  - Help popup background container is a little short, the content is spilling out
  - I would argue that the search button should dismiss all popups
